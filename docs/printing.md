# Printing and swell paper

## From SVG to raised print

1. Open the exported tactile SVG (or choose **Print / PDF** in the app).
2. Print on plain paper at true page size: the SVG is pure black, no greys, sized in millimetres for the paper you selected (Letter, Tabloid, or braille page).
3. Photocopy onto swell paper (also called microcapsule paper).
4. Run the sheet through an emissions fuser (for example a PIAF machine). The black ink absorbs heat and puffs up: 1.5 mm named-part edges and silhouette, 0.8-1.0 mm detail, 0.6 mm leaders, braille dots to Library of Congress Spec 800 dimensions.

## Tips

- The on-screen grey label pills do **not** print: the exported page is pure black.
- The 3D relief view is a design aid; judge spacing there, not by screen brightness.
- If a page must be embossed on a braille embosser instead, the layouts assume swell paper thicknesses; check line prominence on your device.

## 3D printing

The relief tab exports an **STL** of the plate: raised lines, textures and braille dots at the dimensions the site lays out. Useful for quick prototypes of a diagram that will later be re-run on swell paper in bulk.
