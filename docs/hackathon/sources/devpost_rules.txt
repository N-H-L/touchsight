                    Our hackathon is dedicated to providing a safe and comfortable environment and a harassment-free experience for everyone. Any notice of harassment and misconduct regarding gender identity and expression, race, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, ethnicity, nationality, religion, political views, intellectual experience, and other topics at the discretion of the directors will result in your project being DISQUALIFIED.
    •    Teams of 1-4 (inclusive) consitiing of any students are allowed to submit projects as a group but must be indicated that each member did work and submit on Devpost as a group. 
    •    All work must be done during the hackathon. Violation of this rule will result in potential consequences.
    •    You may repeat an idea you previously worked on, but you may not re-use code or other materials and you may not submit a previous project.
