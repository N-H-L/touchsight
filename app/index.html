TouchSight

Textbook diagram in, tactile graphic out.

Runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Make a diagram readable by touch in under a minute

Teachers of blind students redraw science diagrams by hand: removing clutter, swapping print for braille, keeping lines far enough apart to feel. TouchSight reads the diagram's own labels, follows each leader line to the part it names, segments that part, and lays out a swell-paper-ready graphic with braille labels following BANA tactile graphics guidelines.

    4. Export

    Print on plain paper, then photocopy onto swell paper and run it through a fuser (e.g. PIAF). Braille dots are drawn at Library of Congress Spec 800 spacing.

    TouchSight is a hackathon prototype for Banana Hacks 2026. Uses Tesseract.js (OCR), SlimSAM via transformers.js (segmentation) and OpenCV.js, all running locally. Labels use uncontracted UEB braille. Source and credits.
